- **LLM Fine-Tuning** -- Adapt language models fi speak wi language, understand wi history
- **Diffusion Models** -- Generate Caribbean art, understand the math behind AI creativity
- **RAG Systems** -- Build knowledge systems grounded in Caribbean facts and culture

### Production & Deployment
- **MLOps & Deployment** -- Ship models in the Caribbean reality: limited bandwidth,

### Capstone Projects

- **Smart Caribbean Island** -- Build an AI system fi manage energy, water, tourism,

## Philosophy

Wi nah just learning AI fi get a job at a big tech company abroad (though dat is
an option). Wi learning AI fi **build solutions right here** in the Caribbean.
Every lesson connects to a real Caribbean problem. Every project could become a
real Caribbean business.

Di Caribbean has unique challenges -- island geography, hurricane exposure, small
markets, connectivity issues. But these challenges are also **opportunities**.
Solutions we build here can serve island nations worldwide, from the Pacific to
the Indian Ocean.
