## 🌍 Caribbean Context Throughout

Every single example in dis section use REAL Caribbean context:

- **Agriculture** — Sugarcane, cocoa, nutmeg, banana yield prediction (Jamaica, Guyana, Barbados, Trinidad, Grenada)
- **Tourism** — Hotel reviews, visitor recommendations across all islands
- **Climate** — Hurricane prediction, rainfall patterns, sea temperature data
- **Music & Culture** — Reggae, soca, dancehall, calypso, reggaeton, kompa, chutney classification
- **Economics** — GDP, trade, tourism revenue clustering for CARICOM nations
- **Sports** — Cricket analytics, Usain Bolt sprint prediction, track & field
- **Healthcare** — Disease classification, dengue patterns
- **Marine** — Coral reef health, fishing patterns
- **Energy** — Solar and wind potential across di islands
