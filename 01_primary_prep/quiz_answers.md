## Lesson 1: What Is AI? — Quiz Answers

**Question 1:** What does AI stand for?
- **Answer: B) Artificial Intelligence**
- "Artificial" means made by people, "Intelligence" means di ability fi think and learn!

**Question 2:** How does AI learn?
- **Answer: B) By looking at lots and lots of examples**
- Just like how yuh learn fi read by seeing lots of words, AI learn by seeing lots of examples!

**Question 3:** Which of these is an example of AI?
- **Answer: C) A phone assistant like Siri dat can answer yuh questions**
- Siri, Google Assistant, and Alexa all use AI fi understand what yuh say and answer yuh!

**Question 4:** If yuh show a computer 500 pictures of ripe mangoes and 500 pictures of green mangoes, what can it learn fi do?
- **Answer: B) Tell yuh if a NEW mango is ripe or green**
- Dis is called "image classification" — di AI learn from examples and then can recognize new ones!

**Question 5:** What is di difference between a regular computer and an AI computer?
- **Answer: C) AI computers can learn and get better, regular computers just follow exact instructions**
- Regular computers are like recipe books. AI computers are like chefs who can taste and adjust!

## Lesson 2: Patterns Everywhere — Quiz Answers

**Question 1:** What is a pattern?
- **Answer: B) Something dat repeat in a way yuh can predict**
- Patterns are repeating things — once yuh see di pattern, yuh can guess what come next!

**Question 2:** In which month do di MOST hurricanes happen in di Caribbean?
- **Answer: C) September**
- September is di PEAK of hurricane season. Dat is when di ocean water warmest and conditions right fi hurricanes.

**Question 3:** A sprinter runs: 14.0 seconds, 13.5 seconds, 13.0 seconds. What might dem next time be?
- **Answer: C) 12.5 seconds (faster)**
- Di pattern shows dem getting 0.5 seconds faster each time! So 13.0 minus 0.5 = 12.5!

**Question 4:** Why does AI need fi find patterns?
- **Answer: B) So it can predict what might happen next**
- Patterns help AI make predictions — whether it rain, who going win di match, or what song yuh want fi hear next!

**Question 5:** What comes next? 🏏⚽🏏⚽🏏___
- **Answer: B) ⚽ (football)**
- Di pattern alternates: cricket, football, cricket, football, cricket... so next is FOOTBALL!

**Yuh Score:**

## Lesson 3: Sorting and Classifying — Quiz Answers

**Question 1:** What is di difference between sorting and classifying?
- **Answer: B) Sorting is putting tings in order; classifying is putting tings into groups**
- Sorting = arranging in order (small to big, A to Z). Classifying = grouping similar tings together (all fruits in one group, all vegetables in another).

**Question 2:** If yuh sorting mangoes by size, which group would a guinep go in?
- **Answer: C) Small**
- Guinep is a small fruit! It much smaller dan a mango or breadfruit.

**Question 3:** Which Caribbean country is known as "Di Spice Isle"?
- **Answer: C) Grenada**
- Grenada grows nutmeg, cinnamon, cloves, and more. Dem even have nutmeg on di flag!

**Question 4:** How does Spotify use AI classification?
- **Answer: B) It classify songs by tempo, mood, instruments fi recommend music yuh might like**
- Spotify AI analyze every song and group dem by speed, mood, instruments, genre, and more. Den it match yuh listening habits fi suggest new tunes!

**Question 5:** Yuh have dese items: cricket bat, football, cricket ball, football boots, cricket pads. How would yuh classify dem into two groups?
- **Answer: B) Cricket equipment in one group, Football equipment in another**
- Cricket group: bat, ball, pads. Football group: football, boots. Dat is classification!

**Yuh Score:**
- 5/5 = Sorting Superstar! 🏆
- 3-4/5 = Great classifying skills! 🌟
- 1-2/5 = Try sorting some tings at home fi practice! 📚

## Lesson 5: My First AI — Quiz Answers

Di quiz fi Lesson 5 is built into di Python program! But here are di answers
in case yuh want fi check:

**Quiz Question 1:** What does our weather AI use fi make predictions?
- **Answer: B) Data from yuh answers to questions**
- AI always start wid DATA (information). In our case, yuh answers about clouds, wind, and humidity are di data!

**Quiz Question 2:** When is hurricane season in di Caribbean?
- **Answer: B) June to November**
- Six months of di year! September is di peak month.

**Quiz Question 3:** In our AI, what does a HIGHER rain score mean?
- **Answer: B) More chance of rain**
- Di higher di score, di more signs of rain we found. More evidence = stronger prediction!

**Quiz Question 4:** What is di FIRST step in making an AI?
- **Answer: B) Collect data (information)**
- Before AI can do ANYTHING, it need data. No data = no AI! Dat is why we asked yuh 6 questions first.

**Quiz Question 5:** Why did wi give "dark clouds" a HIGHER score dan "morning dew"?
- **Answer: B) Because dark clouds are a STRONGER sign of rain**
- In AI, dis is called "weighting" — some information is MORE important dan others. Dark clouds (score 3) are a much stronger sign of rain dan morning dew (score 1)!
